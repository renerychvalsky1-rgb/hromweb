Prečo lamaWhy a llama
„Lama" sa kedysi hovorilo tomu, kto hru nevedel hrať. "Lama" is what gamers used to call the player who couldn't play.

My to slovo otáčame. V účtovníctve si lama? Presne preto ho nemáš robiť ty. Zapneš lamu, ktorá to hrá za teba — a hrá to dobre. Byť v niečom lama prestalo byť problém. Stalo sa to dôvodom. We flip the word. Clueless about accounting? That's exactly why you shouldn't be doing it. You switch on a llama that plays it for you — and plays it well. Being a "lama" at something stopped being a problem. It became the reason.

Prácu spraví ona. The llama does the work.

AI Only, nie AI First.AI Only, not AI First.

lamaon od prvého dňa stavajú a prevádzkujú AI agenti. Nie je to vrstva na starej firme — je to celá firma. Používame AI a netajíme to. lamaon has been built and run by AI agents since day one. Not a layer on an old firm — the whole firm. We use AI and we don't hide it.

Zákon 7 dní.The 7-day law.

Každý nový produkt má funkčné MVP do siedmich dní. Buď to beží, alebo to nerobíme. Problém je pomalosť, nie chyba — chybu priznáme vetou a vyriešime ďalšou. Every new product ships a working MVP within seven days. Either it runs, or we don't do it. Slowness is the problem, not mistakes — a mistake gets admitted in one sentence and fixed in the next.

Staviame pre EÚ.Built for the EU.

Európske firmy, európske pravidlá, európske termíny. Faktúry, podania a dáta tak, ako ich vyžaduje tvoj štát, nie Silicon Valley. European businesses, European rules, European deadlines. Invoices, filings and data the way your country requires — not Silicon Valley.
